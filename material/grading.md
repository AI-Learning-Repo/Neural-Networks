# Assessment

Assessment combines practical programming skills, collaboration, and individual understanding.

The course uses group activities, pair programming, coding assessments, and feedback.

An alternative assessment track is available for those who do not participate in pair programming.


**Overall Grade Thresholds**

- Grade 5: 93%
- Grade 4: 83%
- Grade 3: 75%
- Grade 2: 67%
- Grade 1: 61%

**Weight Distribution**

- **Group Activities in class**: 28%  
- **Pair Programming in class**: 38%  
- **Exam** : 27%  
- **Feedback**: 7%  
- **Bonus** Up to 100 additional points (equivalent to 10 percentage points), added to the standard assessment score.
