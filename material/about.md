# About the Course

## Welcome

Welcome to the Neural Networks course!

In this course, you will learn how neural networks work, how they learn from data, and how to apply them to different machine learning problems.

The course combines theory with hands-on programming, experiments, and collaborative activities.

The **Neural Networks course** focuses on understanding and working with neural networks, while the **Project course** focuses on engineering neural-network-based systems into useful AI products.

## Mindset

Learning neural networks is not simply about learning a particular library or framework.

You need to understand the underlying concepts, learn how to experiment with models, interpret results, debug problems, and make appropriate technical decisions.

The emphasis is on **learning by doing**. You will first encounter concepts through theory and demonstrations, then apply them through programming activities and pair programming.

You are encouraged to explore, experiment, ask questions, and learn from mistakes.

## Course Description

We begin with artificial neurons and Multilayer Perceptrons (MLPs), then progress toward deeper neural networks and more advanced architectures.

Throughout the course, you will learn to build, train, evaluate, and interpret neural networks using Python and Keras.

The main topics include:

- Multilayer Perceptrons (MLPs)
- Deep Neural Networks (DNNs)
- Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs)
- Recurrent Neural Networks (RNNs)
- Transformers
- Large Language Models (LLMs)

We will closely follow selected materials from MIT's [Hands-On Deep Learning](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/15-773-hands-on-deep-learning-spring-2024/) course, complemented by our own exercises and activities.

## Learning Outcomes

By the end of this course, you will be able to:

- Explain how different neural network architectures work and understand their mathematical foundations.
- Prepare and process data for neural network training.
- Build and train neural networks for classification and numerical prediction.
- Select suitable architectures, activation functions, and training methods.
- Evaluate model performance, interpret results, and identify common problems.
- Apply neural networks to tasks such as image classification and natural language processing.
- Use documentation and experimentation to investigate unfamiliar neural network architectures and tools independently.

## Prerequisites

- Data Processing and Machine Learning

## Teaching Methods

The course combines short theory sessions, demonstrations, hands-on exercises, group activities, and pair programming.

You will receive preparation materials in advance. Classroom time focuses on applying concepts, experimenting with models, solving problems, and discussing results.

## Course Mechanics

- **Class activities:** Any activities not completed during class are expected to be completed at home.
- **Pair programming:** Pair-programming sessions will run from **Week 2 through Week 7**.
- **Self-study:** You are expected to review theory, watch companion lectures, complete exercises, and prepare for upcoming sessions.

## Course Workload

The course is worth **5 ECTS credits**, corresponding to approximately **135 hours of work** (27 hours per ECTS credit).

Over eight weeks, this amounts to approximately **17 hours per week**, including classroom sessions and independent study.

- Classroom sessions: 6 hours per week
- Independent study: approximately 11 hours per week


> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Workload and Support**
>
> The expected independent workload is approximately 10–11 hours per week.
>
> If you find yourself consistently spending significantly more time than this, or struggling to keep up with the activities, contact the teacher immediately so we can identify the problem and find a solution.

## Assessment

Assessment combines practical programming skills, collaboration, and individual understanding.

The course uses group activities, pair programming, coding assessments, and feedback.

An alternative assessment track is available for those who do not participate in pair programming.


**Overall Grade Thresholds**

- Grade 5: 93%
- Grade 4: 83%
- Grade 3: 75%
- Grade 2: 67%
- Grade 1: 61%

**Weight Distribution**

- **Group Activities in class** – 28%  
- **Pair Programming in class** – 38%  
- **Coding Exam**  – 27%  
- **Feedback** – 7%  
- **Bonus** Up to 100 additional points (equivalent to 10 percentage points), added to the standard assessment score.

## Materials

## Principles for Using AI

You may use AI tools to support your learning and programming activities.

However:

- **Understand your code:** You should be able to explain what your code does.
- **Verify results:** Review, test, and evaluate AI-generated solutions.
- **Learn actively:** Use AI to explore concepts and alternatives, not simply to complete exercises without understanding them.

You remain responsible for the code and solutions you submit.

## Where to Go Next

This course provides a foundation for exploring neural networks and deep learning beyond the classroom.

After completing the course, you should have the knowledge and confidence to independently investigate topics such as:

- More advanced neural network architectures
- PyTorch, JAX, and other deep learning frameworks
- Large Language Models (LLMs) and their applications
- New deep learning techniques and research

**The goal is not to master every framework or architecture, but to develop the understanding and skills needed to continue learning independently.**
